## About Me

I build and evolve SaaS products with a focus on **Software Engineering**, **Systems Architecture**, **Automation**, and **AI-Augmented Development**.

I enjoy turning complex requirements into software that is organized, maintainable and easier to evolve as the product grows.

Today, I work on real products such as **DojoMetrics** and **SIGEA**, combining product development with architecture, authentication and authorization, database design, automation, testing and technical documentation.

I use Artificial Intelligence as an engineering accelerator — not as a replacement for technical understanding and decision-making.

> **Technology should reduce complexity, not create it.**

## Engineering Principles

- Architecture before code.
- Automation before repetition.
- Simplicity is a feature.
- AI augments engineers.
- Software should remain easy to evolve.
- Quality is never accidental.
